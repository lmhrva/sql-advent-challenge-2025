Frosty wants to know how many unique snowflake types were recorded on the December 24th, 2025 storm. Can you help him?

Tables
snowfall_log(flake_id, flake_type, fall_time)

FIRST TRY:
select count(distinct flake_type)
from snowfall_log
where fall_time = to_date('12-24-2025', 'mm-dd-yyyy');

RESPONSE:
You're very close! Your use of COUNT(DISTINCT flake_type) is spot on for counting unique snowflake types.
One small thing to watch out for: the fall_time column is a timestamp, and you want to filter by the date part only. Using fall_time = to_date(...) might not work as expected because it compares the full timestamp, including time.
Think about how you can extract just the date from the timestamp to compare it properly.


ANSWER:
select count(distinct flake_type)
from snowfall_log
where fall_time < to_date('12-25-2025', 'mm-dd-yyyy')
    and fall_time > to_date('12-23-2025', 'mm-dd-yyyy');
-- 8

THAT'S CORRECT!

FOLLOW UP:
Learn how to extract a date from a timestamp to make this better.
